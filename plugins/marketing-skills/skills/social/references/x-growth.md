## Profile Audit

Audit before any growth work. The profile converts the profile clicks the algorithm rewards.

**Bio**
- [ ] First line says who you help and how
- [ ] One specific niche, not "entrepreneur | thinker | builder"
- [ ] A proof element: title, metric, or brand association
- [ ] A link: newsletter, product, or site
- [ ] No hashtags
- [ ] 100-160 characters

**Pinned post**
- [ ] Exists and gets refreshed about monthly
- [ ] Shows your best work or strongest hook
- [ ] Has a clear next step: follow, subscribe, read

**Last 30 posts**
- [ ] At least one post a day
- [ ] A mix of posts, threads, replies, and quotes
- [ ] Replies are 30% or more of activity
- [ ] Engagement trend: improving, flat, or declining (see [performance-analysis.md](performance-analysis.md))

## Post Types by Growth Job

**Threads: most reach, best follow conversion.**
- Post 1 is the hook: a specific claim in the first 8 words.
- Post 2 gives context or the promise.
- Posts 3 to N carry one idea each, readable on their own, 3-4 lines at most.
- The last post summarizes, restates the hook for fast scrollers, and asks for the follow.
- 5-12 posts: under 5 feels thin, over 12 loses people. One weak post drags the whole thread, so cut it.

**Single posts: breadth.** Shapes that travel: an observation ("[Thing] is underrated, and the reason is [specific]"), a list ("10 tools I use daily"), a contrarian take, a lesson ("I did [X] for [time]. The biggest lesson:"), a framework in 30 seconds. One idea per post. Put links in the first reply, not the post.

**Quote posts: authority.** Add what the original missed: data, a counterpoint, or experience that confirms or contradicts it. Never just "This." Quote engagement scores twice.

**Replies: the fastest path to visibility.**
- Reply to accounts 2-10x your size.
- Add value; your reply is content and should stand as a post.
- Be early on large accounts, within the first 30 minutes (see [listening.md](listening.md) for finding the posts).
- Insightful replies get quoted, which is free reach.

## Cadence by Account Size

| Followers | Posts a day | Threads a week | Replies a day |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| Under 1K | 2-3 | 1-2 | 10-20 |
| 1K-10K | 3-5 | 2-3 | 5-15 |
| 10K-50K | 3-7 | 2-4 | 5-10 |
| 50K+ | 2-5 | 1-3 | 5-10 |

Space original posts about 60 minutes apart. These are practitioner defaults, not platform rules: test them against your own numbers.

Where the effort goes by stage:
- **Under 1K:** about 70% replies to larger accounts, 30% your own posts.
- **1K-10K:** 2-3 threads a week, daily engagement, and a recurring series.
- **10K+:** repurpose across platforms and grow a newsletter from the audience.

## 60-Day Growth Plan

**Weeks 1-2: foundation**
1. Fix the bio and pinned post.
2. Pick 20 accounts in the niche to engage with daily.
3. Write 10-20 value-adding replies a day to larger accounts.
4. Post 2-3 single posts a day, testing different formats.
5. Publish one thread.

**Weeks 3-4: pattern recognition**
1. Review which formats earned the most engagement.
2. Double down on the top two.
3. Move to 3-5 posts a day.
4. Publish 2-3 threads a week.
5. Quote relevant posts daily.

**Month 2 onward: scale**
1. Build 3-5 recurring series (for example, a weekly framework post).
2. Repurpose threads into LinkedIn posts and newsletter sections.
3. Build reply relationships with 5-10 accounts your size.
4. Try audio spaces if your niche uses them.
5. Review follower growth and engagement monthly with `scripts/social_metrics.py`.

## Competitor Research on X

1. Find 5-10 accounts in the niche with strong engagement. X's advanced search operators (such as `min_faves:100` with a topic) surface high-performing posts.
2. For each, record posting frequency, formats, hook patterns, and engagement.
3. Extract: how top posts open, the 3-5 themes that earn the most engagement, the format mix, posting times, and what draws replies versus likes versus reposts.

The full method is in [reverse-engineering.md](reverse-engineering.md); compare accounts on a per-follower basis with [performance-analysis.md](performance-analysis.md).

## What Else Kills Reach

On top of the list in [x-algorithm.md](x-algorithm.md):
- Links in the post body instead of the first reply
- More than 1-2 hashtags
- Tagging people who won't engage back
- Irregular posting: steady daily presence beats occasional bangers

## Common Pitfalls

1. **A weak first post in a thread.** If the hook doesn't stop the scroll, nothing after it matters.
2. **Only broadcasting.** Replies and engagement are half the growth work.
3. **A generic bio.** "Helping people do things" tells nobody anything.
4. **Copying formats without adapting them.** What works in one niche on X doesn't transfer as is.
