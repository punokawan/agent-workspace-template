# Launch Tiers and Cross-Team Timeline

A tiered plan for launches that involve several teams (product, marketing, sales, customer success):
how to pick the tier, a week-by-week timeline per tier, the launch metrics board, and the post-launch
report. It extends the major / medium / minor split in **Ongoing Launch Strategy** with timelines.
Scale every item to the team: a solo founder runs the same steps in fewer hours.

## Contents

- Choose the Tier
- Tier 1: Major Launch (6-8 weeks)
- Tier 2: Standard Launch (3-4 weeks)
- Tier 3: Minor Launch (1 week)
- Launch Metrics Board
- Post-Launch Report

## Choose the Tier

| Tier | Typical scope | Prep time | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New product, product line or major capability | 6-8 weeks | Prospects, customers, press, partners |
| 2 | Significant feature, strategic integration, pricing or packaging change | 3-4 weeks | Customers and selected prospects |
| 3 | Small feature or improvement | About 1 week | Existing customers |

**Tier 1** if any is true: net-new product or product line; a large share of the period's pipeline or
revenue goal depends on it; press or analyst coverage is expected; competitors are likely to respond.

**Tier 2** if any is true: a top customer request; a new integration with a strategic partner; a
pricing or packaging change.

**Tier 3** otherwise.

Set the budget from the goal and the channels (see **marketing-plan**), not from the tier label.

## Tier 1: Major Launch (6-8 weeks)

### Foundation (weeks -8 to -5)

**Kickoff (week -8)**
- [ ] Kickoff with product, marketing, sales and customer success
- [ ] Goals with numbers: pipeline or revenue, signups or qualified leads, coverage, adoption in 90 days
- [ ] Owners for each workstream: launch lead and messaging, feature readiness and demo environment,
      campaigns, content, sales enablement, support readiness
- [ ] Shared timeline in the team's project tool
- [ ] All owners confirm goals and dates

**Strategy (week -7)**
- [ ] Positioning and messaging (see **product-marketing**)
- [ ] Channel plan across owned, rented and borrowed channels (see **The ORB Framework**)
- [ ] Target segments and personas
- [ ] Budget by channel
- [ ] Press release drafted, embargo date set

**Content (week -6)**
- [ ] Landing or product page with the primary conversion (demo, trial, signup)
- [ ] Announcement post
- [ ] New sales deck slides
- [ ] Social graphics and short video or demo recording
- [ ] Announcement and nurture emails (see **emails**)

**Enablement (week -5)**
- [ ] Battle card updates, demo script, FAQ, objection answers (see **sales-enablement**)
- [ ] Sales and support training scheduled
- [ ] Beta customers recruited for quotes and case studies
- [ ] Sales can demo the feature without help

### Preparation (weeks -4 to -1)

- [ ] Campaign tracking: UTMs, attribution, CRM campaign (see **analytics**)
- [ ] Teasers on owned channels
- [ ] Press and analyst briefings under embargo
- [ ] Partner co-marketing confirmed (see **co-marketing**)
- [ ] Webinar or live event set up, if used (see **events**)
- [ ] Paid campaigns ramping, if used; landing page headline tests running (see **ads**, **ab-testing**)
- [ ] Preview for top customers
- [ ] Full dry run: pages, forms, CRM routing, in-app messages
- [ ] Launch-day runbook with owners and times
- [ ] Customer success briefed; help docs updated; support staffed for launch day
- [ ] Final sign-off on every asset

### Launch (weeks 1-4)

**Launch day**
- [ ] Press release and direct pitches
- [ ] Email to the relevant lists; in-app announcement to existing users
- [ ] Social posts; community and partner posts
- [ ] Paid at full planned budget, if used
- [ ] Sales outreach to priority accounts
- [ ] Metrics checked every few hours: traffic, signups or demo requests, pipeline, coverage, errors

**Days 2-7**
- [ ] Daily funnel review; fix the worst step first
- [ ] Fast lead follow-up (agree the response time with sales)
- [ ] Press and analyst follow-ups
- [ ] Webinar or live demo
- [ ] Early customer quotes published
- [ ] Pause underperforming paid campaigns

**Weeks 2-4**
- [ ] Post-launch content: use cases, customer stories
- [ ] First win-loss interviews on launch deals (see **sales-enablement**)
- [ ] Budget moved from weak to strong channels
- [ ] Weekly status report against goals

### Post-launch (weeks 5-12)

- [ ] Retrospective: what worked, what didn't, what to change
- [ ] Positioning updated from customer feedback; log it in the context file
- [ ] Sustaining campaigns planned
- [ ] Final report against goals, including return on spend
- [ ] Assets archived for reuse

## Tier 2: Standard Launch (3-4 weeks)

**Weeks -4 to -3**
- [ ] Feature, audience and key messages defined
- [ ] Product page or landing page updated
- [ ] Announcement post and email drafted
- [ ] Sales deck updated; short sales briefing

**Weeks -2 to -1**
- [ ] Campaign tracking set up
- [ ] Social posts scheduled; limited paid budget, if used
- [ ] Pages and forms checked
- [ ] Customer success notified

**Launch week**
- [ ] Email, post, social, in-app notification
- [ ] Sales mentions it in active deals
- [ ] First metrics checked

**Weeks +1 to +2**
- [ ] Metrics analyzed and pages optimized
- [ ] Customer feedback collected; learnings logged

## Tier 3: Minor Launch (1 week)

**Days -5 to -3**
- [ ] Changelog entry, help docs, in-app message copy
- [ ] Support team briefed

**Days -2 to -1**
- [ ] Feature checked in staging; copy approved; in-app message scheduled

**Launch day**
- [ ] Feature live; in-app message on; changelog published

**Days +1 to +3**
- [ ] Support tickets and adoption watched; issues fixed
